## What AGPL-3.0 means in practice

AGPL-3.0 is a strong copyleft license. If you use SmartAccess in your application:

- You **must** make the complete source code of your application available to your users
- This applies whether you distribute the app (App Store, enterprise distribution) **or** serve it as a network service
- You cannot remove or alter the copyright notices
- You cannot sub-license under different terms

If any of these constraints do not work for your business, you need a Commercial License.
